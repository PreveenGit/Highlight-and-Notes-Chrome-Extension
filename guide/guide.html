Practice article

The habit of the margin

For centuries, readers kept a pencil nearby. They underlined the lines that surprised them and wrote small notes for their future selves in the margins.

Marking a sentence is a tiny decision: this matters. That decision is what makes an idea easier to find, and easier to remember, a week later.

Some passages raise a question instead of answering one. Mark those too, so you can come back and learn more when you have time.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Why doesn't the toolbar appear on some pages?

      Chrome doesn't let extensions run on chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store or the built-in PDF viewer. If you just installed Highlight, reload pages that were already open.

      How do I move my highlights to another computer?

      In the library, open the ⋮ menu and choose Export backup. On the other computer, choose Import backup and pick that file. Highlights you already have aren't duplicated.

      What happens if a page changes?

      Highlight remembers the words around each highlight, so it usually finds it again even when the page has changed a little. If it can't, the library shows it as "not found on the page right now".

      How do I remove a highlight?

      Click it on the page and choose the bin icon, or hover it in the library or the toolbar popup and delete it there. Deleting from the library can be undone.

      Can I turn off the toolbar that appears when I select text?

      Yes. Click the toolbar icon, open Settings (the gear) and switch off "Show toolbar when selecting text". You can still highlight with the keyboard shortcut.
