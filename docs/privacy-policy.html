Privacy policy

Highlight (Chrome extension) · Effective November 28, 2026

Highlight lets you highlight text on web pages and keeps a library of those highlights. This policy explains what the extension stores and where. In short: everything stays on your computer. The extension has no servers, sends nothing over the network, and contains no analytics, ads or tracking.

What the extension stores

Only what you create, and only when you create it:

DataWhy
The text you highlight, plus up to 32 characters before and after itTo find the same text and highlight it again when you return to the page
The address (URL) and title of each page where you made a highlightTo know which page a highlight belongs to and to list pages in the library
Each highlight's color, position in the page text, the time you made it, and whether you marked it as a bookmark or to learn moreTo show it the way you made it and to sort and filter the library
Any note you write on a highlightTo show it with the highlight and let you search your notes
For passages you mark to learn more: the question you write, when you mark it learned, and what you write down that you learnedTo show your To learn and Learned lists and let you search them
Your settings (default color, whether to show the toolbar)To remember your preferences

The extension does not record your browsing history, keystrokes, form contents or any page you have not highlighted on. It reads a page's text only on your device, to draw and restore your highlights.

Where it is stored

All data is kept in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced to your Google account and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

The extension does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than the highlighting features described above.

Export and import

When you choose Export, the extension creates a file (Markdown or JSON) and Chrome saves it where you choose. When you choose Import, it reads the file you pick. What happens to those files afterwards is up to you.

Permissions

  • Read and change data on the websites you visit — to draw highlights on pages and restore them when you come back.
  • Storage and unlimited storage — to keep your highlights and settings on your device, even large libraries.
  • Side panel — to show your highlight library beside the page.
  • Favicons — to show each page's icon in the library (Chrome provides these locally).

Deleting your data

Delete single highlights or whole pages from the page, the popup or the library. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from Chrome.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
